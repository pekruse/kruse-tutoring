Services

Choose a session around the exam, experiment, or project you’re working on.

Exam prep

Bring a practice exam and a test date. Work through timed problems, identify recurring mistakes, and leave with a study plan.

Lab-data review

Bring your measurements and expected results. Check units, plot the data, and investigate why the experiment and model disagree.

Circuits & simulation

Walk through your schematic, predict voltages and currents, and compare your calculations with a Multisim simulation.

Arduino project troubleshooting

Bring a sketch, wiring diagram, and the behavior you expected. Trace one signal at a time and build a short list of tests to isolate the fault.

Python & MATLAB code review

Bring a script that gives an unexpected result. Trace it with a small test case and check the output by hand.

Technical presentation practice

Rehearse a lab or project talk. Get feedback on your diagrams, explanation of results, and answers to likely questions.
