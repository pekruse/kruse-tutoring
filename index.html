From the circuit notebook

Where did the voltage go?

Two resistors in series share a 9 V supply.

9 V × (2 kΩ / 3 kΩ) = 6 V

The 2 kΩ resistor takes twice the voltage drop of its 1 kΩ neighbor. The two drops add back up to 9 V.
